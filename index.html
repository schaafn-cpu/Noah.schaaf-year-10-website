Welcome to Skateboard World!

Skateboarding is a fun activity where people ride on a board with four wheels. People skateboard for fun, exercise and to learn new tricks.

Why do people skateboard?

People skateboard for lots of different reasons. Some people enjoy learning tricks, while others like riding around with their friends.

  • It is fun and exciting.
  • You can learn new tricks.
  • You can meet new people.
  • It helps improve balance and coordination.

Basic Skateboarding Safety

Safety is important when skateboarding. Beginners should wear a helmet and other protective gear. It is also important to practise in a safe area.
