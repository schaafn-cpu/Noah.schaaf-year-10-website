Street Skateboards

Street skateboards are used for tricks such as kickflips and ollies. They are common at skateparks and in places with rails and ledges.

Longboards

Longboards are longer than normal skateboards. They are good for travelling and cruising around.

Cruiser Skateboards

Cruiser skateboards are made for comfortable riding. They usually have softer wheels, which help when riding on rough surfaces.

Which one should you choose?

  • Street skateboard: for tricks.
  • Longboard: for longer rides.
  • Cruiser: for riding around town.

Remember to choose a board that suits what you want to do and always wear a helmet.
